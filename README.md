🏬 Walmart Retail Performance & Operational Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, 3-page Power BI analytics project designed to help retail managers look beyond top-line revenue, uncover operational bottlenecks, and make data-driven decisions on store performance, customer loyalty, and product line strategy 📊.

📌 Problem Statement & Project Overview

In high-volume retail operations like Walmart, relying strictly on top-line revenue can mask underlying operational inefficiencies, such as poor store staffing during peak hours, underperforming product tiers, or low customer loyalty retention.This project analyzes transactional retail data across three specialized executive views to optimize store performance, customer experience and inventory management:-

1.Branch Dashboard (Store Revenue & Foot-Traffic Overview): Tracks macro-level revenue metrics, store quadrant performance, peak shopping hours, and branch category distributions.
2.Customer Dashboard (Segmentation, Payment & Rating): Analyzes profitability per customer tier, satisfaction scores, gender-wise category preferences, and payment method adoption.
3.Product Dashboard (Performance & Revenue Report): Maps sales volume across time windows, product line revenue contributions, price bracket sensitivity, and category satisfaction trends.

💡 Business Impact & Key Insights:-

⏳ Optimized Staffing & Operations: Hourly transaction analysis shows concentrated revenue peaks from 2 PM to 6 PM. Reallocating staff schedules and opening additional checkout lanes during these hours reduces wait times and checkout abandonments.
🎯 Targeted Store Interventions: Scatter quadrant positioning reveals which branches generate lower basket sizes despite equal foot traffic, guiding targeted bundling strategies for lower-performing branches.
💳 Loyalty & Gross Margin Optimization: Membership data highlights that Elite and Prime tiers drive the highest gross income contribution ($15.38K). Personalizing rewards around preferred payment channels (e.g., E-wallet and Credit Card) enhances customer retention.
